Every one of the 141 Redux misses now has an observation from the pinned original XL. The original ran 100 for 120 iterations. Of the 34 with missing advertised files, the original rejected 31 exact mission selections and returned an empty member lookup for three. Seven more had an unverified simulation. The additional 63 cases came from 36 independently hash-verified distributions and contributed 54 original starts. All 16 light-index and all 14 segment-function cases ran in the original, as did 15 of 16 animation cases and 11 of 15 unsupported-format cases. These are concrete targets for engine work. All 64 now meet the startup criterion. [Full counts by family](../XL_compat/evidence/campaign-startup-census-20261119.json) · [Original eleven-case map](xl_expanded_readiness_recovery_20261116.md)

Count every advertised distribution, level, authored mode and supported platform. Mark a campaign path complete when a player can take its authored branches, return from secrets, reach its published ending and continue after death and a cold save restore. Mark a multiplayer mode complete when two players can join, score, respawn and finish under its own win condition, with both peers agreeing on the result. Verify rendering, audio and input at those same gameplay events on Android and desktop. Where a distribution lacks a file, identify the complete release separately and retain the original entry.

The catalog contains 625 distributed descriptors, 121 negative ordinals and 74 distributions explicitly declaring competitive-only play. There are 1,236 distinct candidate mine byte strings. Addons, incoming player state and outgoing branches can give two copies different behavior. Transfer a result across packages once equivalence is demonstrated for the behavior being claimed. ## Architecture decision

Prioritize a dedicated Android XL backend trial. The original engine already supplies the coupled systems these games need, including mission resources, trigger scheduling, physics, AI, saves and replication. Keep Redux as the comparison implementation. Have one engine own a mission from start to finish, with its own saves and compatible network peers. Compare a rendered level, Android input, an earned exit, background/resume and a cold restore. Then compare a completed and scored two-peer match.

The pinned original builds and runs on desktop. Its arm64 NDK configuration stops at desktop GLEW. Two original missions exercised the same eleven GL function names without a direct match in GLES3 or the Redux shim. Both also used fixed-function state and quads. Desktop trace replay reproduced their reference pixels. An original-engine Android frame, mobile frame times, running audio and device lifecycle remain open. XL 2 has a different renderer, save format and AI. Pumo's author identifies the Arbruijn line for this release. [Desktop measurements](../XL_compat/evidence/original-android-render-scope-20261119.json) · [XL 2](https://www.descent2.de/d2x) · [Pumo's reference engine](https://pumosoftware.com/d2x-xl/)

Estimate remaining tranches after completing a published campaign branch and a scored match, then repeating each with another author. Record time spent fixing engine behavior, authoring controls and qualifying devices separately. Until then, startup progress supplies no defensible tranche count for full support.

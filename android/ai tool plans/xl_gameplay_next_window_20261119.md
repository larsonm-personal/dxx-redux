## How to qualify a release

| Question                                                | Evidence to collect                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | Decision it supports                                                                                                                                                |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Can the selected engine run the published distribution? | Pin the archive, descriptor, mine and effective addon definitions. Record the author's intended engine version, game mode, entry and ending. Attempt exact selection on the reference.                                                                                            | Resolve an incomplete package or record an unavailable original. Count a complete alternate release separately.                                                     |
| Can a player finish the authored objectives?            | Start with a new pilot. Cross required doors, acquire keys, operate switches, fight, rescue or score as appropriate. Earn every required destination and secret return, including the campaign ending. Check a death/respawn and a fresh-process restore at consequential points. | Qualify each route and mode. Keep an unreachable exit, a lost pickup or a divergent save attached to the last earned checkpoint.                                    |
| Does the same play work on the supported hardware?      | Compare reference captures at the switch, combat, exit and restore. Verify lighting, models, HUD, music, positioned audio, menus and touch/controller input. Record frame-time distributions and stalls at a fixed resolution on a low-end and a representative physical phone.   | Identify rendering or device-specific cost before adopting the backend. Agree on the frame-time and memory limits for each advertised device tier before measuring. |
| Can the mode complete under network play?               | Record both peers' resource identities, scores, objective ownership, death/respawn, join state, match ending and return to the lobby. In co-op also record the level transition.                                                                                                  | Qualify the specific mode and compatible peer versions. Repeat on a second author's map and exercise reconnect where supported.                                     |

Keep separate counts for advertised entries, runnable releases, earned campaign endings, scored modes and qualified device tiers. Publish the remaining entries with their exact missing file, first failing operation or untested dimension. A full-support claim needs every applicable cell qualified or an explicit, user-visible limit on supported releases and platforms.

### Idle deaths and archive precedence

The stop-frame receipt separates completed frames from the frame on which the pilot was found dead. Headless Redux completed 82 frames on Wolfpak 3 level 6 and stopped on 83. Each of the three byte-identical Pyrotechnica copies completed 104 and stopped on 105. Isolated robot and projectile traces agreed across the three copies. The original 60 Hz diagnostic used a constant 1,092 fixed-point units per tick. Redux distributed the remainder over 1,092- and 1,093-unit ticks. The original counter stopped at 82 and 90 respectively. These totals use different terminal conventions. [Receipt changes](https://github.com/larsonm-personal/dxx-redux/pull/786)

A second pinned-original observation used the same fractional clock as Redux. Every common tick had identical elapsed time and delta, 82/82 on Wolfpak and 88/88 on each Pyrotechnica copy. Wolfpak fired its first projectile on original tick 24 and Redux tick 29. Its first shield loss was on ticks 63 and 72 respectively. Pyrotechnica fired on ticks 21 and 23 and lost shields on ticks 55 and 59. Matching the clock moved the original Pyrotechnica observation from 90 updates to 88. The remaining fire and damage differences are present with equal time steps. All four runs retained their ordinary idle-pilot outcome. In Wolfpak the first shooter was robot 48 in object slot 69. Its position first diverged on tick 23. In Pyrotechnica the first shooter was robot 47 in slot 107, and its position first diverged on tick 21. Both shooters began in the same room at identical coordinates across engines. At the first AI observation, the pilots occupied the same room with a 25-unit raw fixed-point difference on one axis.

Fight for Your Life's original HOG has two different replacements named `pdl10.hxm` for level 3. The differing byte is the boss flag for robot definition 45. A direct archive lookup selected different physical rows in the pinned lookup and PhysicsFS. The actual installed definitions in flight agreed. Robot 45 had boss flag 0 and robot 52 had boss flag 25 in both engines. Real ordinals 2, 3 and 4 completed all 120 frames in both engines. Two Belial archives and the two distinct level members in Descend Again also had duplicate names. For the Belial level 13 HXM and both differing Descend Again levels, direct pinned and PhysicsFS lookups selected identical bytes. A change to generic archive precedence has no demonstrated benefit for these flights.

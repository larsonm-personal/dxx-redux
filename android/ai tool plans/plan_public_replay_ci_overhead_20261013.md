# Public replay CI overhead

## Scope

Measure the existing thirteen-scene native public gates before making a focused
optimization. Preserve all true-headless and headed repeats, wrong-oracle and
bad-input controls, imported gameplay and diagnostic comparisons, strict native
oracles, natural exits, and immutable corpus inputs. Keep retail coverage intact.

## Approach

1. Record exact-head CI phase timings and local cold/warm runtime, wrapper and
   trace-validation measurements using the same verified binaries and inputs
2. Optimize only a demonstrated overhead without weakening process isolation or
   evidence; retain complete raw artifacts and separately accessible summaries
3. Run relevant admission, mutation, wrapper and live-engine checks and compare
   reports against the baseline; obtain independent review before publication
4. Stack on the verified public matcen segment, publish a focused draft and
   monitor the final exact-head checks without merging; later gameplay segments
   can build on this independently reviewed optimization

## What a player can now do

In Sapphire Wolf's Black Prophecy beta, the desktop pilot earned all three keys on level 2, rescued three hostages and reached the secret entrance in room 632. It entered the first secret, operated switch 4, collected the energy pickup in room 282 and exited through room 544. Redux returned the player to room 386 on level 2 with four lives, 59,900 points, all three keys, three hostages and just over 136 energy. Nine switches retained their operated state after a separate process loaded the returned checkpoint. The previous build returned the player to the same room and lost those switches. Secret restore had reset the incoming XL world's clock. A stock Descent II return still exercised the native zero-clock rule. Mixed-format and signed-ordinal controls exercised the same decision. The round trip has no manual save between entering the secret and returning. Earlier Pumo flights earned level 4, collected the Metillis blue key and continued combat. Kritical Mass on the Android emulator retained two operated switches, four kills, 400 points and three lives through cold restore. Campaign endings, physical-phone qualification and competitive match endings remain open. [Pumo and Kritical Mass](https://github.com/larsonm-personal/dxx-redux/pull/638)

The ordinary x86_64 Android emulator cold-loaded that earned return from the complete authored mission package. With sound and rendering active it recovered room 386, the nine switches, 59,900 points, all three keys, all three hostages, four lives and the energy brought back from the secret. This exposed two portability bugs in Redux. Its model and audio readers now recognize an owned logical source when only its path capitalization differs. A second script continued in the same live process when the prompt interrupted waypoint steering. Redux restored room 386 with all nine switches, 59,900 points, four lives, three keys, three hostages and 136.049896 energy. The phone wrote its own quick save and secret companion. After a process stop and restart, that pair restored the same state in 13.585 seconds of automation. The corrected native approach covered 613 steps across 81 chained processes in 4,885.44 seconds. Its return and independent cold reload took 141.96 seconds.

# Original Tulev1 guard269 ordinary-combat owner

## Scope

Register one assisted-local native owner after the original Tulev1 master5/blue-key owner. It consumes that owner's actual versioned blue-key save, makes fourteen ordinary held Laser bursts from a declared local pose, requires the tracked guard's actual death and player kill credit, settles all owned shots, then writes one new versioned save. A separate fresh process cold-restores that save without advancing a frame or writing another save.

This is a bounded local combat segment, not connected travel or the switch12 route. It uses no Guided shot, invulnerability, resource grant, enemy suspension, or direct trigger call. The bounded wall-contact observer may truncate; the contract checks that its lower bound and truncation status are internally consistent without claiming a complete contact list.

## Source and verification

The runner uses the existing pinned preview executable and guarded retail/resource inputs. It retains the predecessor's immutable-save, receipt, trace, and process identity checks and adds a distinct receipt nonce per phase. A checked-in asset-free template declares the actions; the runner binds the copied predecessor save by hash. The combat checker requires fourteen paired player-owned Laser bursts at the declared cadence, unchanged Guided inventory, tracked target identity, damage beyond binding shields, a negative target shield state, kill credit, a living player, and no owned shots at save time. The cold checker preserves the saved gameplay state and permits only source-documented process-local diagnostic resets.

Public verification consists of the asset-free contract's rejection controls and the exact public test catalog and omission/skip policy checks. Native assets, saves, receipts, traces, and measurements remain private. This plan makes no FAST/readiness claim and does not claim the native owner has passed on this branch.

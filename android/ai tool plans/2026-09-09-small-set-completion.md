## Guided-missile skepticism and future priorities

User direction: guided missiles must not become a catch-all for routing we have not figured out. The main campaigns are expected to have no required guided-missile shots. Treat any new such classification there as a likely planner, visibility, or state-model defect to investigate, not as an accepted exception. This does not reject the existing custom-mission findings without evidence

- A failed ordinary-shot search is not proof that a guided missile is required. A missile-sized geometric path only establishes a possible candidate, not necessity or executable flight
- First investigate ordinary access and alternatives: trigger ordering, doors that reopen or can be shot from the reverse face, reachable off-center firing positions, actual projectile radius/transparency, alternate targets and routes, and stale or incomplete live wall state
- Separate evidence for "guided shot is feasible" from evidence for "this objective requires a guided shot". Record what ordinary alternatives were checked and what remains unknown. Search exhaustion, a timeout, or a partial route remains unresolved evidence
- Require a reachable launch pose, legitimately obtainable equipment/ammunition, and native flight respecting turning, speed, lifetime, and actual target activation before calling a guided action executable. Even that does not establish necessity if an ordinary alternative remains
- Future regression coverage should flag new required-guided annotations in the main campaigns for investigation. Do not promote search-budget exhaustion into a weapon requirement or count an unsupported guided prediction as a completed level
- These are investigation and future-design constraints. No current metadata classifications or runtime behavior are changed by this note

Suggested next work, in priority order:

1. Close the gap between canonical simulations and live/co-op play: reproduce from captured saves and world state, including keys acquired out of order, already-fired one-shot triggers, closing doors, and player/companion separation
2. Consolidate how canonical planning and live recovery represent door/trigger state, implicit satisfaction versus actual activation, and renewed access requirements. Preserve focused fixtures while replacing duplicated assumptions, rather than accumulating more exceptions
3. Improve failure evidence for ordinary routes: identify the blocking portal or shot, its controlling dependencies, rejected alternatives and the precise physical check that failed. Use this to investigate the remaining non-guided failures before expanding guided support
4. Test robustness beyond a single deterministic run: different starting poses and timings, delayed door crossings and repeat visits, plus recorded-input demo gates for any shared movement/collision change
5. Audit guided predictions with the skepticism above before implementing full equipment collection and flight verification. Keep player passage, companion passage and projectile passage distinct

Counterstrike secret-5 and the previously deferred case remain deferred unless the user changes that priority. Completion of the FVI compatibility repair does not mean the broader all-level routing effort is complete

# Sphere 1.51 packed light sources

The standalone release's third mine has SHA-256 `edea8a6c7c006459739d272665b19a377fd8a091ab8d3c8f456595f5d75c29a2`. Its 22/40 table declares 234 six-byte source rows and 32,769 eight-byte deltas. Fifty-six rows name a segment or side outside the mine. Row 232 names segment 3059 side 4, with four deltas beginning at 32,768. That side and segment 3056 side 5 are opposite ends of an open portal. Both have no wall or texture. Neither appears in the flicker, wall trigger, object trigger or reactor target tables.

At original revision `2576a98b5de310e3ac7c9777df2677d1627b8bae`, `lighting/light.cpp` matches the requested segment and side before using a delta index. Its flicker and trigger paths request real sources. The blowup path in `main/segment.cpp` requires a destructible texture. The original ran this exact mine through 120 frames. Its source loop checks the starting index and assumes that the following count is valid. Redux keeps bounds on the complete span.

For the observed 22/40 layout, the inventory records each malformed, unreachable row's original number, face, count and start in its diagnostics. The runtime receives the other 177 rows and all 32,769 deltas. A malformed span on a textured surface, a one-sided portal, either end of a targeted portal or an authored flicker still fails admission. The classic translation rejects the filtered table. Other layouts retain their existing admission rules.

The ordinary Windows engine loaded the original third mine and executed 120 unassisted frames at 60 Hz. The direct signed-secret load also passed. A separate player fixture started explicitly on level three, used ordinary controls to reach room 2 and witnessed its authored flicker on side 1 lower the room light from 65,535 to zero and raise it again. A fresh process loaded the earned room save and observed both changes again. This fixture records its direct level setup. Campaign entry from level two has its own route.

The Neptune controls exercised the actual changing-light owner and cold restore in a second author's geometry. Both standard and malformed-target cases passed 1,280 pinned reader comparisons, 524 UVL samples and 56 attenuation samples. The inventory boundary tests also exercise an invalid emitting face, authored flicker at either end, valid neighboring records and refusal by classic translation.
